## About & Methods

### About Roman Landscapes

Roman Landscapes is a student atlas by **Jimmy Liu**, with academic and technical guidance from **Fang, Xinjie** and **Classical Cat Digital Humanities Lab**. It brings settlement, transport, economic and historical sources into the same geographic frame.

### Reading evidence on a map

Look at the source, date bounds and position basis for each record. A documented collection location can anchor an archive even when the transactions themselves have no coordinates. Modeled sea lanes describe possible connections. The archaeological and written evidence retains its own units and level of certainty.

### Sources and methods

Cross-source links require a source identifier or documented correspondence. Native fields, bibliography and statement references can be inspected in the detail panel. Each offline release identifies its data by content digest so the complete map and documentation can be read without a connection.
