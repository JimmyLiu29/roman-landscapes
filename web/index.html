Sources & map limits

Made with Natural Earth. The map uses modern coastlines, terrain and watercourses as physical reference points for the Roman world. Ancient shorelines and rivers could follow different courses.

Coverage extends from 17°W–55°E, 15°N–63°N. The 258 relief tiles cover zooms 0–6. The map can be viewed at zooms 2–7, with zoom 7 enlarging existing detail.

The ocean is derived from the land outline. Water features classified as canals or reservoirs by Natural Earth are excluded.
